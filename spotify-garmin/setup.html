Use your own Spotify app

Free, about 2-3 minutes. Follow the 3 steps.

1

Open Spotify for Developers and sign in with your Spotify account. If asked, accept the terms.

Open Spotify for Developers

If it does not open, copy developer.spotify.com/dashboard/create into Safari or Chrome.

2

In "Create app", fill in the form by copying these values:

App nameGarmin Remote
App descriptionRemote for my Garmin watch
Redirect URI (then tap "Add")https://storione90.github.io/spotify-garmin/callback

Tick "Web API", accept the terms and tap "Save".

3

Open "Settings" of the app you just created, copy the "Client ID" and paste it here:

If Spotify then shows an error, open "User Management" in your app and add your own Spotify email.
