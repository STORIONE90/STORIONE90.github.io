Privacy policy – PlaylistRemote

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. Who we are

PlaylistRemote is developed by Filippo Picchiarelli as a private individual.

2. Data collected by the developer

None. The developer does not collect, receive or store any personal data. The app has no servers of its own, no analytics and no advertising.

3. Data used by the app on your watch

To control Spotify, the app signs in to your Spotify account using Spotify's official procedure. The resulting access keys (tokens) and the ID of the Spotify app used are stored only on your watch and are used only to communicate with Spotify.

Only to show them on the watch, the app reads: your playlists (name and number of tracks), the song playing, the artist and the name of the device that is playing. These data are not saved and are not sent to anyone else.

4. Third-party services

Using the app involves these services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Spotify: your account and music playback.
  • Garmin: the Garmin Connect app and the Connect IQ store, which carry data between phone and watch.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts these web pages and may record technical data such as the IP address.
  • PayPal and your bank: only if you choose to make a donation.

5. These web pages

These pages do not use cookies, analytics or advertising. The sign-in guide saves the Client ID you type only in your browser, so it can fill it in again if you retry.

6. How to revoke access

You can remove PlaylistRemote's access at any time from your Spotify account page (spotify.com/account/apps). Uninstalling the app from the watch deletes the saved access keys.

7. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
